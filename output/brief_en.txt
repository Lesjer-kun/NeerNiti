3. DATA LIMITATIONS
  - Units are not printed in the MPPCB reports; standard units (mg/l) are assumed.
  - 174 readings are BDL/ND (below detection limit): treated as very low, not zero, not missing. 61 readings are missing or not sampled: never treated as clean.
  - Bairagarh: no Durga 2025 table in the report; Durga 2025 is not covered.
  - Malikhedi: no Durga 2025 table in the report; Durga 2025 is not covered.
  - Rani Kamlapati / Kamla Park: site-name confidence is medium. Ganesh tables and Durga tables print different names for this site; these may be two places.
  - Only pH, DO and BOD have CPCB Class B limits. COD, solids, turbidity, conductivity and metals are shown for information only. Suspended solids were not tested in every year.
  - Readings are one sample per phase from past festivals. They describe past conditions, not live water quality.

4. NOTE
  This brief compares past MPPCB readings with CPCB Class B limits only. It does not describe what the water will do to people, and it does not describe current conditions. It supports the officer's decision; it does not replace it. Every number above is traceable to a report page in the evidence view.
